07

The Science
of Scent

A scent-driven grooming brand reimagined — modern identity and packaging that honors craft and sensory experience.

Barrister & Mann, a men's grooming brand rooted in the molecular chemistry of scent, needed an identity as unexpected and meticulously crafted as their fragrances.

We delivered a flexible, modern brand identity paired with a cohesive packaging system that feels luxurious yet contemporary. The packaging was inspired by records — where the art is forefront, and the label becomes a canvas to bring each fragrance to life. The logo was derived from the combination of two molecules; this molecular vibration in chemistry is what we create and interpret as scent — a concept that served as a flexible, modular mark that scales across all print and digital needs.

Each element, from typography to messaging, reinforces the brand's sensory sophistication. The result: a distinct, memorable experience that elevated Barrister & Mann above the competition.
